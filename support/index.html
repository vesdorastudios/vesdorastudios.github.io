Support

Something not working, or an idea for a puzzle? Write to us at vesdorastudios@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days. You can write in English, Turkish or Swedish.

It helps to include your phone model, its system version, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and, if you can, a screenshot.

Loop Isles

I bought the Harbor Pass, but on a new phone I see ads again.

Open the Harbor Pass page in the game and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

Progress is stored on your device only; deleting the game deletes it. A device backup that includes app data (iCloud or Google backup) can bring it back when you restore the whole phone. Your Harbor Pass is never lost: restore it as above.

How do I start over?

In the game, open Settings → Reset progress. Your Harbor Pass and settings are kept.

Can I play without ads?

Yes: every level is free, and the Harbor Pass removes the ads (and gives five hints a day). Ads never appear in the middle of a level, and rewarded ads for hints are always optional.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

See the Loop Isles privacy policy for how ads work and how to change your choices in the game and in your device settings.

A level seems impossible.

Every level is checked by a solver and can be finished within its budget. Try the Hint button: it shows the best next route from the bridges you have already built.
