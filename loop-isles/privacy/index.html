Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Loop Isles ("the game") for iOS and Android uses, and why. The game is made by Vesdora Studios, operated by Mahmut Görkem Genç in Sweden ("we", "us"). Questions: vesdorastudios@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress, settings and records are stored only on your device. We do not have a server and we do not receive them.
  • If you play without the Harbor Pass, the game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as described below, subject to the choices you make.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone or tablet, so it works offline:

  • Game progress (finished levels, stars, the level in progress, daily puzzle results, game mode records).
  • Settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion, text size, language) and which guides you have seen.
  • Whether you own the Harbor Pass, cached so the game works without a connection.
  • A small diagnostic log of in-game events (for example "level finished"), kept on the device to help fix problems. It is not sent anywhere.

This information never leaves your device unless your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) includes app data, which is controlled by you and by Apple or Google. Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players without the Harbor Pass may see an ad between levels (never in the middle of a level) and may choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for an extra hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device type, operating system, language and app information,
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped), and diagnostic data.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's privacy options.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised based on it.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. Owning the Harbor Pass removes interstitial ads.

Purchases

The Harbor Pass is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase is valid, so the game can unlock it and restore it. Your payment information is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Loop Isles is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Legal basis (EEA/UK)

Data stored on your device is used to provide the game you asked for. Personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; non-personalised ads and ad measurement rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and Google's own legal bases as described in its policies.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on any server, most requests can be fulfilled directly on your device (by resetting progress or deleting the game). For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use your device settings and Google's tools. You can contact us at any time, and if you are in the EU you may complain to your local data protection authority (in Sweden: IMY, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
