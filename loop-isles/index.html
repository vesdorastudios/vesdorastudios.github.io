Loop Isles

Every delivery needs a way across the night sea. Bridges cost timber, but once built they are free to share. Find the one network that serves them all, with the least wood you can.

What's inside

360 handmade puzzles

Fifteen regions, each with its own twist: rocks, old bridges, shoals, deep channels, chasms, scarce timber. Every level is proven solvable and its best answer is known.

Three game modes

Race the tide against the clock, keep a perfect streak with three lives, or drift through endless puzzles on the open sea.

A puzzle every day

The same daily puzzle for everyone, free, with no streaks to lose.

Calm by design

No energy, no timers in the journey, unlimited undo, and a harbour that fills in as you travel. Plays fully offline, in nine languages.

Free to start

The first four regions (96 levels), the daily puzzle and all game modes are free. The Harbor Pass, a single one-time purchase with no subscription, opens the other eleven regions (264 levels), five hints a day and an ad-free game.
